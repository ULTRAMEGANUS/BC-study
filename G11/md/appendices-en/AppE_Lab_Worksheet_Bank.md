## E-1　LAB SAFTY Station Activity — Full Solutions｜安全站活动全解

*Source｜素材原件：`LAB SAFTY.pdf`（10 站安全站活动 + 情景改错）。The stations show pictures; the answers below cover what each station tests.｜各站配图；以下答案覆盖每站考查点。*

### Station answer key｜各站答案要点

| Station｜站 | Tests｜考查 | Model answers｜参考答案 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Lab area safety 实验区安全 | clutter hazards | ① walkways and benches clear of bags/coats; ② nothing near the balance edge; ③ rule: keep your bench clear and bags under the desk.|台面走道清空、书包放桌下，防绊倒与泼洒。 |
| 2 Lab dress code 着装 | goggles, tied hair, closed shoes, no loose sleeves | goggles ON before chemicals; hair tied; no shorts/open shoes. 安全镜先行、长发束起、不穿露趾鞋。 |
| 3 Handling glassware 玻璃器皿 | broken glass & chipped flask | broken glass → dustpan + **broken glass bin**, never hands; chipped/flask with star-crack → do not use, report. 破玻璃用扫帚入专用箱；有裂痕的烧瓶停用上报。 |
| 4 Chemical safety 化学安全 I | wafting & reading volume | ① smell by **wafting**（扇闻）, never sniff directly; ② beaker read to nearest 10 mL (graduation marks), graduated cylinder to 1 decimal (estimate between marks). 烧杯按刻度估到 10 mL 位；量筒估读到小数一位。 |
| 5 Chemical safety II | no tasting; weighing | ① never taste anything in a lab; ② mass: tare the weigh boat first, then record. 先去皮再称量。 |
| 6 Chemical safety III | reading labels & hazard diamonds | check name + concentration + hazards before use; return with the label facing out. 用前核对标签。 |
| 7 Chemical safety IV | dilution direction | acid **into water**, slowly, with stirring — never water into acid. 酸入水。 |
| 8 Chemical safety V | spill response | alert the teacher first; use spill kit; never wipe acids with bare paper towels by hand. 先报告老师，用应急包。 |
| 9 Fire safety 用火 | hair/clothing, Bunsen flame, passage of people | tie hair, no leaning over flame; blue cone flame for heating; never reach across a flame; know the fire blanket & extinguisher locations. 束发、蓝焰加热、不越火取物、知道灭火毯位置。 |
| 10 Clean-up 收尾 | waste sorting | chemical waste to the labelled container, not the sink; rinse glassware; wipe bench; wash hands. 废液入指定桶、不入下水道；洗手。 |

## E-3　LAB1 Making Observations — Full Solutions｜观察实验全解

*Source｜素材：`LAB1/MAKE OBSERVATION_merge.pdf`（预习题 + 钙与水实验）。*

### Definitions｜定义（预习题背景）

- **Qualitative observation 定性观察**: describes qualities without numbers — colour, state, odour, sound, feel.｜描述性质，不带数字。
- **Quantitative observation 定量观察**: carries a measured number + unit — temperature, mass, volume, time.｜带测量数字与单位。

### Pre-lab answers｜预习答案要点

1. Qualitative examples for this lab: the metal's appearance (silvery/grey, darkening); bubbles forming; the indicator (if present) changing colour; fizzing sound.｜定性例：金属外观变化、气泡、变色、嘶嘶声（至少写两条具体的）。
2. Quantitative examples: water temperature at start/end (°C); volume of water used (mL); mass of calcium added (g); time to a fixed change (s).｜定量例：水温、水量、钙的质量、时间（至少两条，带单位）。

### Observation table discipline｜观察表填写纪律

- Write **before / during / after** rows separately; every quantitative cell needs number + unit + instrument.｜前/中/后分行；定量格必须有数+单位+仪器。
- Never write conclusions (e.g. “it reacted”) inside the observation table — observations first, inference later.｜观察表里不写结论（如“反应了”）——先观察后推断。
- The `中英对照-ans` version of the handout is the bilingual answer copy; use it to check, not to copy.｜中英对照答案版用于核对，不用于抄写。
