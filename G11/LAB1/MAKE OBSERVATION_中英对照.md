## 5. Reference Page 教师参考页 {#sec5}

### Introduction 引言

Observations are an important component in the scientific method, a means whereby scientists solve problems. In chemistry, you will constantly be checking for changes when you do experiments. Observations are classified into two types: qualitative and quantitative.

观察是**科学方法（scientific method）**——即科学家用来解决问题的一套手段——的重要组成部分。做化学实验时，你需要不断地检查变化。观察分为两类：**定性观察（qualitative observations）**与**定量观察（quantitative observations）**。

Qualitative observations tend to be rather general and use words, not numbers, to describe an object or event. Qualitative observations in the lab use your senses of sight, smell, and hearing to describe what you observe.

定性观察往往比较笼统，用**文字（words）**而非数字来描述对象或事件；实验中的定性观察依靠你的**视觉、嗅觉和听觉（sight, smell, and hearing）**来描述所观察到的内容。

Quantitative observations are more specific and usually describe something in terms of numbers. Quantitative observations in the lab require the use of equipment such as thermometers, scales, and a variety of volume measuring glassware.

定量观察更加具体，通常用**数字（numbers）**来描述；实验中的定量观察需要借助**温度计（thermometers）**、**天平（scales）**以及各种**测量体积的玻璃仪器（volume measuring glassware）**等设备。

### Objectives 实验目标

1. To make observations while watching materials interact and undergo change.
   在观察物质相互作用、发生变化的过程中做出观察。
2. To record and classify these observations as qualitative or quantitative.
   把这些观察记录下来，并归类为定性观察或定量观察。

### Procedure: Part I 实验步骤：第一部分（钙与水）

1. Fill a medium beaker with ~150 mL of tap water.
   在一个中等大小的**烧杯（beaker）**中加入约 150 mL 自来水。
2. Fill one test tube with tap water to the brim so that no air remains inside.
   将一支**试管（test tube）**装满自来水直至管口，使管内不留空气。
3. With your thumb covering the open end, invert the test tube into the beaker. Leave the test tube filled with water upside down in the beaker, as in the figure on the right. Make qualitative and quantitative observations on your data table ("Before").
   用拇指按住管口，把试管倒转插入烧杯。让装满水的试管**倒置（upside down）**留在烧杯中（如右图）。在数据表"实验前（Before）"一栏做定性与定量观察。
4. Obtain a piece of calcium. Make qualitative and quantitative observations on your data table ("Before").
   取一块**钙（calcium）**。在数据表"实验前"一栏做定性与定量观察。
5. Place the piece of calcium into the beaker and immediately shift the test tube over to cover the metal. Hold the test tube in place. Record qualitative and quantitative observations on your data table ("During").
   把钙块放入烧杯，并立即把试管移过去罩住金属，按住试管保持不动。在数据表"实验中（During）"记录定性与定量观察。
6. Add 2 drops of phenolphthalein to the beaker and record your observations ("During").
   向烧杯中加入 2 滴**酚酞（phenolphthalein）**，记录你的观察（"实验中"）。
7. When no further changes appear, record qualitative and quantitative observations on your data table ("After").
   当不再出现新的变化时，在数据表"实验后（After）"记录定性与定量观察。
8. All solutions may be poured down the sink with plenty of water.
   所有溶液可用大量水冲入水槽排放。

### Procedure: Part II 实验步骤：第二部分（铝与氯化铜）

1. Fill a medium beaker with ~25 mL of copper (II) chloride. Make qualitative and quantitative observations of the solution on your data table ("Before"). (Copper (II) chloride is poisonous. Do not get any into your mouth.)
   在中等烧杯中加入约 25 mL **氯化铜（copper (II) chloride）**溶液。在数据表"实验前"对溶液做定性与定量观察。（**氯化铜有毒（poisonous）**，切勿入口。）
2. Obtain a piece of aluminum foil and loosely roll it into a tube. Make qualitative and quantitative observations of the aluminum on your data table ("Before").
   取一张**铝箔（aluminum foil）**，松松地卷成筒状。在数据表"实验前"对铝做定性与定量观察。
3. Place the tube of aluminum foil in the beaker containing copper (II) chloride solution as in the figure on the right and immediately make qualitative and quantitative observations on your data table ("During").
   把铝箔筒放入盛有氯化铜溶液的烧杯中（如右图），立即在数据表"实验中"做定性与定量观察。
4. When no further changes appear, record qualitative and quantitative observations on your data table ("After").
   当不再出现新的变化时，在数据表"实验后"记录定性与定量观察。
5. Dispose of both the solution and solid in the beaker labeled "Aluminum + Copper (II) Chloride Waste" in the fume hood.
   把溶液和固体都倒入**通风橱（fume hood）**内标注"铝 + 氯化铜废液（Waste）"的容器中。
6. Wash your hands with soap and water.
   用肥皂和清水洗手。
7. Give your partner(s) a compliment.
   给你的同伴（partner）一句夸奖。
